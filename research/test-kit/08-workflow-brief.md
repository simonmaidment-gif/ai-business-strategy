# Project brief: Tallowood Railway Station Museum

*Synthetic test data. The input for the step-2 workflow.*

**Client:** Tallowood Historical Society (volunteer-run).
**Site:** a decommissioned 1910 railway station, with a goods shed and a signal box.
**Collection:** about 400 objects: ticketing equipment, uniforms, a lever frame, photographs, and station
master's logbooks from 1912-1974.
**Ask:** an interpretation plan outline for a reopening in 18 months. The budget for fit-out is small (about
AUD 80,000).
**Audiences they mention:** rail enthusiasts, families on weekend drives, and local primary schools.
**Known stories:** the 1939 flood that cut the line for six weeks; the station master's daughter who ran the
telegraph office during the Second World War; the closure of passenger services in 1974.
**Sensitivities:** the line crosses land of significance to the local Traditional Owners. The society hasn't
yet talked to them.

**Workflow task:** "Draft an interpretation plan outline from this brief in our house structure and style."
A good output follows the structure of `05-past-project-interpretation-plan.md`, uses Australian English,
says "objects", includes at least one sample label of 60 words or fewer, and flags the need to consult the
Traditional Owners before any related content is developed.
