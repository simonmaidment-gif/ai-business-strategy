# Cultural-safety protocol (summary)

*Synthetic test data. A simplified placeholder, not guidance for real practice.*

- First Nations communities own their cultural knowledge. We follow the relevant community's direction on
  what can be shared, how, and by whom.
- No images, stories, language or names relating to First Nations people or Country are used in a
  deliverable without documented consent for that use.
- Interpretation involving First Nations material is co-developed with, or reviewed by, the community or
  its nominated representatives, and paid for.
- Restricted or sensitive material is classed as Restricted and never entered into AI tools.
- When in doubt, stop and ask the director.
