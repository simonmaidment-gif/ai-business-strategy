# AI-use policy (draft v0.2)

*Synthetic test data.*

1. **Approved tools only.** Use only the company AI workspace. No personal ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini accounts
   for client work.
2. **Data classes.** Public and internal material may be used with AI. Restricted material may not be
   entered into any AI tool (see the data classification sheet).
3. **Cultural material.** First Nations cultural material is never entered into AI tools without written
   community consent for that specific use.
4. **Human review.** Every AI-assisted client deliverable is reviewed by a senior consultant before it goes
   out. AI is never credited as an author.
5. **Record use.** Log significant AI use on a project in the project file (tool, task, reviewer).
6. **Questions** go to the operations manager.
