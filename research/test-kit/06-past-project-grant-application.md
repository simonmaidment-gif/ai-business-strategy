# Grant application: Bellmore Shire collection digitisation (summary)

*Synthetic test data. Prepared by Fieldnote Studio for Bellmore Shire Council, 2025.*

**Programme:** a state collections-care grant (fictional round). **Amount requested:** AUD 48,000.

## Project summary
Digitise and catalogue 1,200 objects from the Bellmore Shire historical collection, held in three volunteer-run
museums, and publish them in a shared online catalogue.

## Need
The collection is catalogued on paper cards, and the volunteers who hold most of the knowledge are ageing.
Two museums have no digital records at all.

## Outcomes
- 1,200 objects photographed and catalogued to a shared standard
- 12 volunteers trained in cataloguing
- A public online catalogue launched by June 2026
