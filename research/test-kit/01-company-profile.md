## Who we are

Fieldnote Studio is a cultural heritage and exhibition consultancy based in Melbourne, founded in 2014.
There are eight of us. We work with regional museums, galleries, historical societies and local councils,
mostly in Victoria and southern New South Wales.

## What we do

- **Interpretation plans:** how a site or collection tells its stories, to whom and through what media
- **Grant applications:** mainly state arts and heritage programmes and philanthropic trusts
- **Significance assessments:** statements of significance for collections and objects
- **Exhibition development:** content, label copy and object lists for small to mid-size exhibitions

## Clients (by type)

About 40% local councils, 30% regional museums and galleries, 20% historical societies and 10%
philanthropic or private collections. Typical project fee: AUD 15,000-60,000.

## How we write

- **Australian English** (colour, organisation, programme).
- **"Objects", never "artefacts"**, in anything visitors read.
- Interpretation text is written for a **reading age of 12**. **Label copy is 60 words at most.**
- Active voice, present tense for objects ("This chair shows..."), past tense for events.
- No superlatives we can't source ("the oldest", "the only").
- First Nations material is handled under our cultural-safety protocol. **Nothing is used without
  community consent.**
- We sign off as "Fieldnote Studio", never as individuals, on client documents.
