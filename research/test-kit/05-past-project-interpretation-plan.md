# Interpretation plan: Wirrin Creek Mill Museum (summary)

*Synthetic test data. Fieldnote Studio, 2025.*

## 1. Purpose
Refresh the interpretation of a restored 1880s flour mill run by a volunteer historical society.

## 2. Audiences
1. Day-trip families from Melbourne (primary)
2. Local school groups, Years 3-6
3. Heritage enthusiasts and returning locals

## 3. Key messages
- The mill fed the district for 70 years.
- Work at the mill was skilled, seasonal and dangerous.
- The building has been rebuilt three times, and each rebuild left traces you can still see.

## 4. Themes and stories
| Theme | Story | Objects |
|---|---|---|
| Grain to bread | Following one harvest through the mill | Millstones, sack trolley, flour sacks |
| Mill families | The Doyle family, millers 1891-1948 | Wages book, photographs |
| Fire and rebuilding | The 1902 and 1931 fires | Charred beam, insurance letter |

## 5. Media
Wall panels, a hands-on grinding station, a family trail booklet and an audio piece with a former miller's
grandson.

## 6. Sample label (53 words)
> **Millstone, about 1885.** This stone ground wheat into flour for more than 40 years. Two stones like
> this turned against each other, one fixed and one spinning. Look for the grooves cut into the surface.
> Millers sharpened them by hand every few weeks with a steel pick. Can you find the pick marks?

## 7. Evaluation
Visitor survey at six months, school-group feedback, and volunteer observations.
