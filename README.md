# AI Business Strategy

Research, writing and code on AI business strategy, built on the AWS Business Strategist certification material.

| Folder | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `resources/` | Source material and an index of it |
| `research/` | Notes, syntheses and competitive/product landscape studies |
| `writing/` | Posts and articles on the field and practical strategy |
| `playbook/` | Playbook for small organisations to boost productivity and sustainability with AI |
| `framework/company-os/` | Open-source Company OS framework (draws on Curatorial.app, GBrain, the AWS CAF-AI, existing products) |
| `consulting/` | Positioning, portfolio and case material for AI strategy consulting work |

## Status

Research phase. The method is in [`research/method.md`](research/method.md). Nothing here is a finished
framework yet: findings are published as research (method, evidence, open questions) and the framework comes
after the evidence supports it.
